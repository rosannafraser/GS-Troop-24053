What the girls actually earn

There is no general list of mandatory badges. The important difference is official earned work versus participation patches.

🏅

Official badges & Daisy petals

Girls complete published requirements. These are official earned awards placed on the front of the uniform. Daisy petals teach the Girl Scout Law; they are official, but not generally mandatory.

🎟️

Fun patches

Recognize attendance, a special event, or a troop experience. They usually have no national requirements and belong on the back of the vest or tunic.

⭐

Highest awards

Separate from ordinary badges. Juniors working toward Bronze and Cadettes working toward Silver complete a level-appropriate Journey before their Take Action project.

One activity, three level-appropriate badges

The girls can participate together, but each level completes and earns its own badge version.
